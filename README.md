**StreamPulse** is a lightweight, open-source browser extension designed for Twitch and Kick viewers. It automates repetitive tasks (like clicking Channel Points) and keeps you updated on your favorite streamers' statuses without changing the chat UI.

It perfectly complements extensions like **BetterTTV (BTTV)**, **FrankerFaceZ (FFZ)**, and **7TV** (which focus on emotes and chat customization).

## ✨ Features

- 🎁 **Auto-claim Twitch Channel Points:** Never miss a bonus point again. Claims are executed in the background without requiring the tab to be active.
- 🏆 **Twitch Drops Auto-Claim:** Automatically claims your Twitch Drops rewards as soon as the progress bar reaches 100%.
- 🔔 **Real-time Live Notifications:** Get instant desktop alerts when a streamer you follow goes live on **Twitch** or **Kick**.
- 🛠 **Player Error Fixer (Error 2000 / 3000):** Automatically reloads the stream if Twitch drops a `#2000` or `#3000` network error.
- 💬 **Chat Filters:** Hide specific keywords, spam, or bots directly from your browser.
- 🛑 **Anti-Pause:** Prevents Twitch from freezing or pausing the stream when you switch to another tab.

## 🚀 Installation

StreamPulse is built for Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi).

1. Go to the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/streampulse-multi-streame/ipfhbfabadbpkjimhdcjadopnahdpddh).
2. Click **Add to Chrome**.
3. Open the extension menu and start using it instantly! No account required.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is it free?**
Yes, StreamPulse is 100% free with no ads.

**Does it replace BetterTTV?**
No! BetterTTV modifies the chat and adds emotes. StreamPulse automates viewer rewards (Points, Drops) and provides notifications. We recommend using both together.

**Are my data private?**
Absolutely. We do not collect any personal data. Your preferences are stored locally in your browser.
